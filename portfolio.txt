🌐 Portfolio Description (Clean + Vision Driven)

Project Title:
JukeFlow – The Wireless Jukebox Experience

Description:
A modern take on the classic jukebox—fully wireless, real-time, and collaborative. JukeFlow allows multiple users to connect to a shared room, queue songs, and listen together in perfect sync, recreating the social music experience for the digital era.

Key Highlights:

- Wireless multi-user music system with real-time synchronized playback

- Collaborative jukebox-style queue with live voting and skip control

- Host-based room management for shared listening experiences

💡 Strong Tagline (use anywhere)

A wireless jukebox that transforms shared music into a real-time and provides interactive experience.
